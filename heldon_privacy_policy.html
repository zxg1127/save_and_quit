Heldon（守）隐私政策

守不收集你的任何数据。没有账号，没有统计，没有广告，没有网络请求。

01这份政策覆盖什么

本政策说明 Heldon（守） iOS 版（以下称「守」或「本 App」）如何处理你的信息。适用于本 App 的全部功能，包括创建守约、系统闹钟提醒、提交证明、守住与失守的结算、链的统计，以及设置页。

一句话概括：你写下的、拍下的、说出的内容，都只留在你自己的设备上，我们既不接收也看不到。

02我们不收集什么

  • 不收集个人身份信息：没有姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、位置、设备标识符的采集。
  • 不接入统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK：App 内没有第三方分析库，也没有自建的上报通道。
  • 不做广告：无广告 SDK、无 IDFA / ATT 追踪、无归因统计。
  • 不发起网络请求：App 未引入任何网络库，不会向我们的服务器或第三方服务器传输数据。
  • 不出售、不出租、不共享任何数据给第三方——因为我们手上没有这些数据。

03你的内容存在哪里

所有由你产生的内容都写入设备本地的应用沙盒数据库（SQLite），并受 iOS 应用沙盒机制保护。它们不会离开这台设备。

关于证明记录：守只保存判定所需的元数据，例如摇了几下、文字有多长。你拍下的照片与说出的话不会被长期保存，也不会上传。

05第三方与系统服务

守不集成任何第三方 SDK（无分析、无广告、无社交、无崩溃上报、无支付）。你通过本 App 使用到的系统能力由 Apple 提供，包括闹钟（AlarmKit）、语音识别、设备端模型与 Siri，这些能力的数据处理受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

如果你在系统中开启了「与 App 开发者共享分析」，Apple 可能向我们提供聚合后的崩溃日志。这类日志由 Apple 生成、默认不含可识别你身份的内容；你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进」中关闭。

06设备备份

守自身不上传数据，但如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或使用电脑备份，iOS 可能把 App 的本地数据一并纳入设备备份。这份备份存放在你自己的 Apple 账户或你的电脑里，由你和 Apple 的备份设置决定，我们无法访问，也无法代为删除。

07保存期限与删除

  • 保存期限：只要你没删除，数据就一直留在这台设备上。
  • 删除方式：卸载 App 会连同数据库一起删除，我们没有副本，也无法为你恢复。
  • 导出：当前版本不提供数据导出，因为所有内容本来就在你的设备上。

08儿童隐私

守不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。由于本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，这一条对任何年龄的用户都成立。

09你的权利

在 GDPR、CCPA 等法规下，你有权访问、更正、删除、限制处理或导出你的个人数据。对守而言，这些权利天然成立：数据只在你手上，你随时可以查看（App 内「全部守约」与「链」页），也可以随时删除（卸载 App）。由于我们不持有任何数据，不存在需要我们代为执行的查询或删除请求。

10本政策的变更

如果守将来新增会接触网络的功能（例如可选的云同步），我们会在功能上线前更新本页并说明变更内容，同时在 App 更新说明中提示。继续使用即表示你接受更新后的政策。

本页顶部的「生效日期」即最近一次修订时间。

11联系我们

对本政策有疑问，或想确认某个功能如何处理数据，请写信到：mement_support@163.com。我们会尽量在两个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy

Heldon Privacy Policy

Heldon collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no network requests.

Effective: November 12, 2026 Applies to Heldon for iOS Bundle ID: vip.mement.heldon

Zero collection No profiles, no ad IDs.
Zero upload No networking code at all.

01What this policy covers

This policy explains how Heldon for iOS ("Heldon", "the app") handles your information. It covers every feature: creating a Hold, the system alarm that enforces it, submitting Proof, settling as Held or Broken, Chain statistics, and Settings.

In one sentence: what you type, photograph or say stays on your device. We neither receive nor can see it.

02What we do not collect

  • No personal identifiers: no name, email, phone number, contacts, location or device identifier.
  • No analytics or crash SDKs: no third-party analytics library, and no reporting channel of our own.
  • No advertising: no ad SDK, no IDFA, no ATT tracking, no attribution.
  • No network requests: the app links no networking library and transmits nothing to us or to third parties.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of data — we hold none to sell.

03Where your content lives

Everything you create is written to a local SQLite database inside the app sandbox, protected by iOS app sandboxing. It does not leave the device.

About proof records: Heldon stores only the metadata needed to settle a Hold — how many shakes, how long the text was. Photos you take and words you speak are not retained long-term and are never uploaded.

05Third parties and system services

Heldon integrates no third-party SDKs (no analytics, ads, social, crash reporting or payments). System capabilities you use through the app are provided by Apple — alarms (AlarmKit), speech recognition, the on-device model and Siri — and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you enable "Share with App Developers" in iOS Analytics, Apple may provide us aggregated crash reports. Those are generated by Apple and do not normally identify you; you can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

06Device backups

Heldon does not upload your data, but if you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, iOS may include the app's local data in that backup. The backup lives in your own Apple account or on your own computer, governed by your settings and Apple's; we cannot access or delete it.

07Retention and deletion

  • Retention: data stays on your device until you remove it.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes the database with it. We keep no copy and cannot restore it for you.
  • Export: this version has no export, because the data is already on your device.

08Children's privacy

Heldon is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, this holds for users of every age.

09Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data. With Heldon those rights are inherent: the data is already yours, visible in "All Holds" and "Chain", and removable by uninstalling the app. Since we hold no data, there is no request for us to fulfil on your behalf.

10Changes to this policy

If Heldon ever adds a feature that touches the network (for example an optional cloud sync), we will update this page and describe the change before that feature ships, and note it in the App Store release notes. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
